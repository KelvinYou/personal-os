# Personal Direction — Active Attention and Public Understanding

> **Status:** Active working direction<br>
> **Owner:** `docs/VISION.md`<br>
> **Audience:** Kelvin and Personal-OS agents<br>
> **Scope:** Weekend design, travel reflection, public writing, portfolio coherence, and AI-assisted thinking

## Human summary

The current direction is not to optimise every hour or to turn every experience into content. It is to move from passive consumption towards active participation:

> **Encounter the world → ask a real question → observe or experiment → notice what changed → leave a useful trace.**

The trace may be a technical note, a photograph, a decision, a conversation, or no public artifact at all. Rest and unstructured enjoyment remain valid outcomes.

The aim is not to discover one permanent passion. Repeated contact with different activities should reveal what creates durable curiosity, willingness to practise, and useful contribution.

## The core loop

```text
World
  ↓
Attention
  ↓
Question
  ↓
Small experiment
  ↓
Surprise, failure, or changed belief
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Artifact, decision, or memory
```

This loop connects four areas that can otherwise look unrelated:

| Area | Active mode | Failure mode to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Weekends | Explore, make, and enjoy deliberately | Turning rest into another performance metric |
| Travel | Use places as observation lenses | Writing a post-hoc story to justify the trip |
| Public notes | Show how a difficult idea became understandable | Publishing polished conclusions without the path |
| AI assistance | Challenge, test, and clarify human thinking | Letting fluent output replace personal judgment |

## Weekend direction

A good weekend can contain three kinds of time:

1. **Exploration** — unfamiliar places, communities, history, art, talks, or activities.
2. **Creation** — a side project, writing, photography, cooking, music, or another small act of making.
3. **Pure enjoyment** — food, rest, play, or time with other people without an improvement objective.

There is no required ratio and no requirement to publish the result. The purpose of exploration is not to manufacture a passion on demand. It is to create enough contact for genuine preference to become visible.

When giving advice about a weekend, preserve all three possibilities. Do not assume that an activity is more valuable merely because it produces a skill or an artifact.

## Travel direction

Travel can be treated as a light field study, but the themes below are prompts rather than conclusions:

| Place | Observation lens | Useful questions |
|---|---|---|
| Chongqing | Complexity, infrastructure, commerce, and adaptation | How does the city make a difficult physical environment workable? How do old neighbourhoods and new businesses coexist? |
| Chengdu | Time, community, lifestyle, and living well | What do people protect time for? Which businesses support a life rather than only maximise throughput? |
| Jiuzhaigou | Nature, attention, silence, and non-consumption | What remains enjoyable when work, shopping, and optimisation are removed? |

The traveller should record observations before forcing a conclusion. The destination is not successful only if it produces a life lesson or a blog post.

For a material travel decision, check:

- financial safety and upcoming obligations;
- genuine curiosity about the place, not only fatigue with ordinary life;
- whether daily living foundations need attention first;
- enough unstructured time to experience the place without turning it into a checklist.

Do not use the number of international trips as a proxy for extravagance. Evaluate the actual trade-off and the user's current financial state.

## Public knowledge and portfolio direction

The portfolio and the public notes serve different jobs:

- **Portfolio:** What can Kelvin ship, operate, and measure?
- **Notes / experiments:** What did Kelvin have to understand, and how did his view change?

The public writing direction is therefore an **understanding report**, not a generic tutorial. The technical depth remains important; the order of understanding changes.

### Recommended article shape

```text
Concrete question or claim
  ↓
Smallest useful experiment
  ↓
Result or surprise
  ↓
Intuition
  ↓
Formal model and technical detail
  ↓
Code, evidence, and reproducibility
  ↓
Limitations and serious counter-argument
  ↓
Before → After: what changed in the author's belief
```

For a quantitative article, a toy experiment must state what it proves and what it does not prove. A vivid opening cannot replace trial accounting, holdout discipline, uncertainty estimates, or a clear distinction between measured results and interpretation.

Keep the professional identity clear. A phrase such as “Things I Had to Understand” can be an editorial layer or series name; it should not replace the information a hiring reader needs within a few seconds.

## AI reference contract

Use this document when the user asks for advice about weekend design, travel as exploration, public writing, portfolio direction, or the role of AI in personal understanding.

### The agent should

- treat active attention as a preference and working hypothesis, not a mandatory productivity rule;
- preserve rest, play, and experiences that produce no measurable output;
- distinguish observed facts, personal interpretations, and hypotheses;
- use travel themes as prompts while leaving room for surprise and unstructured time;
- recommend a small experiment or one pilot rewrite before proposing a global rebrand;
- keep portfolio claims tied to artifacts, measured outcomes, and honest boundaries;
- use AI for counterarguments, structure, simulations, editing, and reader-confusion checks;
- keep the human responsible for the question, evidence selection, judgment, and final claim;
- read `docs/voice-guide.md` before drafting outward-facing prose.

### The agent should not

- equate consumption with failure or creation with virtue;
- assume that a trip is justified because it can later become content;
- force every experience into a career or learning narrative;
- recommend publishing more articles merely to increase volume;
- remove technical caveats for the sake of a cleaner story;
- treat fluent AI-generated prose as evidence of personal understanding;
- turn this document into a timetable, budget, itinerary, or scoring rubric.

## How to tell whether the direction is working

The direction is working when:

- an experience leaves a question that remains interesting after the day ends;
- experiments produce changed beliefs, not only more notes;
- public writing makes the reasoning easier to follow without reducing technical honesty;
- the portfolio and notes together make both capability and judgment visible;
- the system creates more agency without making leisure feel like unpaid work.

The direction needs revision if active weekends consistently feel like obligations, travel is mainly used to rationalise escape, publishing pressure reduces curiosity, or article redesign improves appearance but not reader comprehension.
