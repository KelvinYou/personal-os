Prepare a small menu of ideas you can borrow whenever something catches your eye:

- **Environmental portrait:** stay small in the frame so the surroundings tell the story. Turn slightly sideways, relax your shoulders, and look at something nearby.
- **Walking candid:** take a few natural steps while a companion photographs you. Let your arms move normally; glance sideways if it feels comfortable.
- **Quiet seated portrait:** sit comfortably, rest your hands on your lap or a table, and look toward the scene. No need to smile at the camera.
- **Details and textures:** photograph a colour, shadow, pattern, or small object that interests you. Try moving closer and keeping the background simple.
- **Reflections and silhouettes:** use a reflection or strong backlight when you happen across one; a simple side profile often works well.

Before departure, try the poses once at home and save two or three reference images on your phone. Choose what feels like you. These are options to recall in the moment, with no shot list, schedule, or quota.
