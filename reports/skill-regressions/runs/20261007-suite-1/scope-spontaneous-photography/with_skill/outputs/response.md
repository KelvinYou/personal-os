Prepare a small menu of ideas you can borrow whenever something catches your eye:

- **Person in the scene:** Leave plenty of space around yourself. Stand slightly sideways, relax your shoulders, and look toward whatever caught your attention.
- **Walking candid:** Take a few ordinary steps while a companion photographs you. Look ahead or to the side; let your arms move naturally.
- **Quiet seated portrait:** Sit comfortably, rest your hands on your lap or hold your cup, and turn slightly toward the light.
- **Small details:** Photograph an interesting texture, reflection, meal, or object close up. Try one simple angle with fewer distractions in the background.
- **Playful portrait:** Turn back toward the camera, laugh with your companion, or make a familiar silly expression—whatever feels like you.

Before departure, try two favorite poses once at home and save a couple of reference images on your phone. Treat them as prompts you can forget about whenever the moment is more interesting.
