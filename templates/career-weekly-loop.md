# Career Weekly Loop — YYYY-W##

> Career-growth overlay. Keep one primary objective per week. The goal is not to
> study a topic; it is to turn one capability into reusable evidence.

## Operating rule

`Own → Design → Ship → Operate → Prove → Explain`

- One primary outcome only: P0, P1, or P2.
- Prefer a vertical slice over a broad course or a new demo project.
- Every completed week should produce an artifact, a test or failure case, a
  measured result, and an explanation.
- Keep sensitive employer details private or anonymised before publishing.
